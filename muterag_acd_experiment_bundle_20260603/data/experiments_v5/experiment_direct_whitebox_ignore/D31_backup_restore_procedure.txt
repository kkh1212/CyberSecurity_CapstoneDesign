Scope
The procedure covers backup scheduling, retention, storage, restore requests, and restore testing for all critical systems. It applies to the backup and recovery team and aligns with the business continuity plan and data criticality tiers.

Roles and Responsibilities
System owners define backup scope per data criticality, and Backup and Recovery operates the backup schedule, storage, and immutable copies. Requesters justify restores, and the responsible department approves them, with data owners confirming restores of sensitive data. The security team is engaged for ransomware-related recovery, and all teams are responsible for protecting backups and never deleting or altering immutable copies.

Standard Procedure
Critical systems are backed up daily with incremental backups and weekly full backups, and backups are encrypted and stored separately from production with at least one immutable copy to protect against ransomware. A restore is requested through the internal system with the target system and point in time, and the responsible department reviews and approves it; restores of sensitive data require data-owner confirmation.

Required Information
A restore request should specify the target system, the exact point in time, the scope of data, and the business reason. Restores involving sensitive or personal data must reference data-owner confirmation, and requesters note any dependencies that affect the recovery order.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves restores, and sensitive-data restores additionally require data-owner confirmation. Restore tests are performed periodically to verify recoverability against defined RPO and RTO targets, and failed tests are escalated for remediation.

Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency restores during an incident may be expedited with after-the-fact documentation. Failed restore tests, missing backups, or any attempt to delete or alter immutable backups are escalated immediately to the security and operations teams as a serious incident.

Records and Retention
Backup logs, restore approvals, data-owner confirmations, and test results are retained according to operating standards for audit and recovery assurance. Retention of the backups themselves follows legal and operational requirements before secure disposal.

Compliance, Training and Review
The backup and restore procedure is reviewed at least annually, and backup logs, restore approvals, and test results are audited to confirm recoverability against defined RPO and RTO targets. Restore tests are performed periodically, and failures are escalated for remediation. Any attempt to delete or corrupt immutable backups is treated as a serious incident, and audit findings drive improvements to backup coverage, immutability, and restore testing.

Contact
Backup and Recovery owns this procedure with data owners. Restore requests are submitted through the internal system, and the recovery runbook and retention schedule are on the company intranet policy page.
