Scope
Reviews cover applications, privileged roles, and shared resources for all employees and contractors. The procedure applies to managers and resource owners who attest to access and to the identity governance team that runs the review cycles.

Roles and Responsibilities
Managers and resource owners attest to the access their reports and resources hold, confirming, modifying, or revoking each entitlement. Identity Governance runs the review cycles, tracks completion, and escalates overdue attestations. The responsible department approves the resulting changes, and the security team investigates clearly inappropriate access discovered during a review. Employees cooperate by explaining continued business need where asked.

Standard Procedure
Standard access is recertified semi-annually and privileged access quarterly, with managers and resource owners receiving an attestation task listing each user's entitlements. Reviewers confirm, modify, or revoke each entitlement through the internal system, and the responsible department approves the resulting changes; access for departed employees is disabled immediately.

Required Information
An attestation requires the reviewer to act on every listed entitlement, confirming continued business need or marking it for change or removal. Where access is retained, a brief justification may be requested, and any entitlement no longer aligned to the user's role must be flagged for revocation.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department approves the changes resulting from each review, and entitlements marked for removal are revoked promptly. Unactioned reviews escalate to the next management level so that no attestation is left incomplete.

Exceptions and Escalation
Where a reviewer needs more time for a complex resource, a short, tracked extension may be granted. Discovery of clearly inappropriate or dormant access during a review is escalated for immediate revocation and, if misuse is suspected, investigation by the security team.

Records and Retention
Attestations, decisions, and revocation records are retained for audit according to operating standards, providing evidence that access is regularly validated. The review calendar and coverage are maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
Recertification is itself a core compliance control, run semi-annually for standard access and quarterly for privileged access, with completion tracked and overdue reviews escalated. Attestations and revocation records provide audit evidence that access is regularly validated, and metrics on coverage and timeliness are reported to management. Dormant or inappropriate access is removed promptly, and findings refine the scope and cadence of future reviews.

Contact
Identity Governance owns this procedure with managers and resource owners. Attestation tasks are delivered through the internal system, and the review calendar and guidance are on the company intranet policy page.
