Scope
The procedure covers all privileged and administrative accounts on servers, network devices, databases, and cloud platforms, and the service accounts with elevated rights. It applies to administrators, engineers, and the privileged access management (PAM) team.

Roles and Responsibilities
Engineers request just-in-time elevation for specific tasks rather than holding standing rights, and the responsible department approves each elevation. The Privileged Access team operates the PAM system, vault, and session recording, while the security team reviews privileged activity. Service-account owners ensure secrets are vaulted and rotated, and all privileged users are accountable for actions taken under their named identities during brokered sessions.

Standard Procedure
Standing administrative rights are minimized; instead, users request just-in-time elevation for a defined task and time window through the internal system, and the responsible department reviews and approves it. Privileged sessions are brokered through the PAM system with full session recording and keystroke logging, and vaulted privileged passwords are rotated automatically after each use or on a schedule.

Required Information
An elevation request should specify the target system, the task to be performed, the required privilege level, and the time window needed. Shared administrative accounts are avoided where individual accountability is possible, so requesters use their named identity for the brokered session.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves just-in-time elevation, granting access only for the requested window. Service-account secrets are vaulted and never embedded in plaintext, and any extension of privileged access requires a fresh approval.

Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency elevation during an incident may be granted with expedited approval and is reviewed afterward. Any attempt to use privileged access outside the approved window or to disable session recording is treated as a serious security incident and escalated immediately.

Records and Retention
Elevation approvals, session recordings, and credential rotations are retained according to operating standards for audit and investigation. Recordings of privileged sessions are protected and access to them is itself logged, with retention following compliance obligations.

Compliance, Training and Review
Privileged-access practices are reviewed at least annually, and elevation approvals, session recordings, and credential rotations are audited to confirm minimal standing privilege and full accountability. Recordings are retained and access to them is logged. Attempts to bypass brokering or disable recording are treated as serious incidents, and audit findings drive reductions in standing access and improvements to vault rotation and just-in-time workflows.

Contact
The Privileged Access team owns this procedure with the security function. Requests are submitted through the internal system, and the PAM onboarding guide and vault usage instructions are on the company intranet policy page.
