Scope
The guide applies to all internal and third-party applications that support federation, and to the application owners and identity engineering team responsible for integrations. It complements the access management and MFA policies.

Roles and Responsibilities
Application owners request and configure SSO integrations and supply accurate metadata and attribute requirements. Identity Engineering reviews and approves integrations, configures attribute mapping, and applies conditional access, while the security team advises on sensitivity-based policies. Once live, application owners keep their integration metadata current, and all users authenticate through the central identity provider rather than local application passwords.

Standard Procedure
New applications integrate using SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect, and legacy password logins are migrated to SSO where supported. An application owner submits an SSO onboarding request through the internal system with the application's metadata and required attributes, and the identity team reviews and approves the integration and configures attribute mapping; integrations are tested before go-live.

Required Information
An onboarding request should include the application name and owner, the federation protocol, the service metadata, the required user attributes and group mappings, and the data sensitivity of the application. Conditional access expectations based on that sensitivity should be stated so appropriate policies are applied.

Approval or Review Process
The identity team reviews and approves the integration, validates login, group mapping, and logout in a test tenant, and applies conditional access policies before production. Changes to attribute mappings later follow the same review process.

Exceptions and Escalation
Applications that cannot support modern federation may use an approved gateway or a time-bound exception with compensating controls. Any registration that appears to request excessive attributes or unexpected redirect URLs is paused and escalated to the security team.

Records and Retention
Integration configurations, attribute mappings, and approvals are retained according to operating standards to support audit and troubleshooting. Test results and go-live approvals are recorded, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
SSO integration practices are reviewed at least annually, and integration configurations, attribute mappings, and approvals are audited to confirm least-attribute sharing and correct conditional access. Test results and go-live approvals are recorded. Registrations requesting excessive attributes or unexpected redirect URLs are paused and escalated, and audit findings drive migration of remaining password-based logins to federation and tightening of conditional access policies.

Contact
Identity Engineering owns this guide with the security team. Application owners submit onboarding requests through the internal system, and the SSO request form and integration checklist are on the company intranet policy page.
