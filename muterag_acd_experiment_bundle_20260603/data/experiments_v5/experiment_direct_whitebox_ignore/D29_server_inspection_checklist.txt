Scope
The checklist applies to all production servers and the operations engineers who perform scheduled inspections. It aligns with the patch, backup, and change management standards.

Roles and Responsibilities
Operations engineers perform scheduled inspections, record results, and propose remediation, and the responsible department reviews findings and approves remediation or accepted risks. Infrastructure Operations maintains the checklist and baseline, and the security team is engaged when findings suggest compromise. Server and service owners are responsible for closing deviations within agreed timeframes so production systems stay healthy and secure.

Standard Procedure
Inspections are performed on a scheduled cadence, and engineers verify patch level, running services, disk and memory usage, backup status, time synchronization, and security hardening against the baseline. Findings are recorded in the internal system, and remediation for deviations is reviewed and approved by the responsible department; accepted risks are documented with an expiry date.

Required Information
An inspection record should capture the server, the date, and the result of each checklist item, noting any deviation from the baseline. Critical items such as patch level and backup status must be explicitly confirmed, and deviations include the proposed remediation.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews findings and approves remediation plans, and accepted risks are documented with an owner and expiry. Critical findings such as failed backups or missing patches are escalated immediately rather than waiting for the next cycle.

Exceptions and Escalation
A short, documented grace period may be granted for low-risk deviations with a remediation date. Repeated deviations trigger a deeper review of the server's configuration, and tampering with inspection results or logs is treated as a security incident.

Records and Retention
Inspection results, remediation actions, and accepted-risk records are retained according to operating standards for audit and trend analysis. Records support capacity planning and are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The inspection checklist is reviewed at least annually and as the baseline evolves, and inspection results, remediation actions, and accepted-risk records are audited for completeness and timeliness. Critical findings such as failed backups or missing patches are tracked to closure, and repeated deviations trigger deeper configuration reviews. Tampering with inspection results or logs is treated as a security incident, and findings drive baseline and process improvements.

Contact
Infrastructure Operations owns this checklist. Inspection records are filed through the internal system, and the checklist template and baseline definitions are on the company intranet policy page.
