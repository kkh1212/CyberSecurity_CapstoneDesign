Change Management Procedure

Purpose
This procedure ensures that changes to IT services are assessed, approved, and tracked to reduce risk and downtime. A consistent change process protects availability and provides an auditable history of what changed and why.

Scope
The procedure covers standard, normal, and emergency changes to IT services and infrastructure and applies to all teams making such changes. It works with the deployment and infrastructure change procedures and the Change Advisory Board (CAB).

Roles and Responsibilities
Requesters describe changes with impact and rollback plans, and the responsible department assesses risk and approves routine changes, escalating significant ones to the Change Advisory Board. IT Service Management maintains the procedure and change calendar, coordinating conflicting changes. Implementers execute approved changes and complete post-implementation reviews, and all teams are responsible for not making changes without the required assessment and approval.

Standard Procedure
A change request is submitted through the internal system with a description, impact analysis, and rollback plan, and the responsible department reviews the risk and approves it, escalating significant changes to the CAB. Approved changes are scheduled to minimize service impact, communicated to stakeholders, implemented, verified, and closed with a post-implementation review.

Required Information
A change request should include the change description, the impact and risk analysis, the rollback plan, the proposed schedule, and the affected services and stakeholders. Significant or high-risk changes provide enough detail for CAB assessment.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department assesses risk and approves routine changes, while significant changes require CAB approval. Conflicting changes are coordinated to avoid overlap, and unsuccessful changes are rolled back and analyzed.

Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency changes follow an expedited approval with a mandatory post-implementation review. Changes implemented without the required assessment or approval are treated as unauthorized and escalated for review and remediation.

Records and Retention
Change records, risk assessments, approvals, schedules, and reviews are retained according to operating standards for audit. The change calendar is maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The change management procedure is reviewed at least annually, and change records, risk assessments, approvals, and reviews are audited to confirm that changes are controlled and traceable. Emergency changes are checked for mandatory post-implementation reviews, and unauthorized changes are detected and escalated for remediation. Conflicting-change coordination is verified, and audit findings refine risk criteria and CAB thresholds so that change-related risk and downtime are minimized.

Contact
IT Service Management owns this procedure with the CAB. Change requests are submitted through the internal system, and the change calendar and assessment guidance are on the company intranet policy page.
