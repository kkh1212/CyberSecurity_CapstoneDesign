Business Travel and Corporate Card Policy

Purpose
This policy defines how employees plan business travel and use the corporate card so that travel is cost-effective, properly approved, and accurately settled. It protects company funds and provides a clear, auditable process for trip expenses.

Scope
The policy applies to all employees who travel for business or hold a corporate card, covering pre-approval, booking, card use, per diems, and settlement. It works alongside the expense reimbursement procedure for non-card expenses.

Roles and Responsibilities
Travelers plan trips, obtain pre-approval, and submit accurate settlements with receipts. Department heads approve travel in advance, and Finance Operations reviews settlements, maintains the per-diem table, and reconciles card statements. Managers ensure travel is necessary and within budget, and the corporate-card program team manages card issuance and limits, with all cardholders responsible for using the card only for legitimate business expenses.

Standard Procedure
All business travel requires pre-approval from the department head specifying purpose, destination, and estimated cost, and bookings should use the company travel portal for negotiated rates. The corporate card is used only for legitimate business expenses, and after the trip the employee submits a settlement request with itemized receipts through the internal system, which the responsible department reviews and approves.

Required Information
A travel request should include the purpose, destination, dates, and estimated cost, and the settlement must include itemized receipts, the business purpose of each expense, and the cost center. Receipts must be submitted within five business days of return, and personal charges made by mistake must be flagged and repaid.

Approval or Review Process
The department head approves travel in advance, and the responsible department reviews and approves the settlement against per-diem limits and policy. Expenses above set limits require additional justification, and cash advances are issued only in exceptional, pre-approved cases.

Exceptions and Escalation
Last-minute essential travel may be approved verbally and confirmed in writing shortly afterward. Missing receipts require a documented explanation and may be partially reimbursed, and suspected misuse of the corporate card is escalated to finance and, if needed, to Human Resources.

Records and Retention
Travel approvals, receipts, settlements, and per-diem calculations are retained according to operating standards for audit. Card statements are reconciled against settlements, and records are kept in line with finance and tax retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The travel and corporate-card policy is reviewed at least annually, and approvals, receipts, and settlements are audited against per-diem limits and card statements. Late or incomplete settlements are followed up, and unusual spending patterns are reviewed. Suspected misuse or fraudulent receipts are escalated to finance and Human Resources, and audit findings drive updates to per-diem tables, limits, and the settlement process to keep travel spending controlled and transparent.

Contact
Finance Operations owns this policy with department heads. Employees submit travel and settlement requests through the internal system, and the per-diem table and travel portal link are on the company intranet policy page.
