Software Installation Approval Procedure

Purpose
This procedure governs installing software on company endpoints and servers so that only licensed, vetted software runs in the environment. Controlling installation reduces malware risk, license exposure, and unsupported configurations.

Scope
The procedure applies to all company endpoints and servers and to every employee and contractor who needs software beyond the managed catalog. Standard productivity software is available from the catalog without individual approval.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees request non-catalog software with a business justification, and the responsible department reviews licensing and security before approval. Endpoint Engineering maintains the catalog, allowlist, and deployment tooling, and the security team advises on vetting. Change management governs admin-level installations through a change ticket, and all users are responsible for not installing unapproved software or bypassing endpoint controls.

Standard Procedure
For software outside the catalog, an employee submits an installation request through the internal system with the business need and license details, and the responsible department reviews and approves it after a security and licensing check. Requested software is checked against the allowlist, scanned for known vulnerabilities, and verified for a legitimate publisher signature, and approved software is deployed through endpoint management where possible.

Required Information
An installation request should include the software name and version, the vendor and license type, the business justification, and the systems where it will run. Software requiring administrative privileges also needs a change ticket referencing the request.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves requests after the security and licensing checks, with results communicated within one to two business days. Admin-level installations require the linked change ticket, and unauthorized installations are removed automatically.

Exceptions and Escalation
Time-limited pilots of unlisted software may be approved for evaluation under restricted conditions and an expiry date. Software that fails the security or licensing check is rejected, and attempts to bypass the allowlist or install unapproved software are escalated to the security team.

Records and Retention
Requests, approvals, license records, and deployment results are retained according to operating standards for audit and license management. Allowlist changes are recorded, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The installation procedure is reviewed at least annually, and requests, approvals, license records, and deployment results are audited for licensing compliance and security. Endpoints are periodically scanned for unauthorized software, which is removed automatically, and allowlist changes are recorded. Attempts to bypass the allowlist or install backdoor software are escalated as incidents, and audit findings drive catalog updates and tighter vetting so only licensed, vetted software runs in the environment.

Contact
Endpoint Engineering owns this procedure with the security team. Employees submit requests through the internal system, and the managed application catalog and request form are on the company intranet policy page.
