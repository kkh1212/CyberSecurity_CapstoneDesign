Scope
The FAQ applies to all front-line support staff handling customer inquiries and complements the detailed support handling procedure. It covers intake, verification, response approval, and escalation.

Roles and Responsibilities
Front-line support staff log inquiries, verify customer identity, and respond using approved templates, escalating sensitive or complex cases. The responsible department reviews or approves sensitive responses, and Customer Support maintains the FAQ and templates. The security team is engaged for suspected impersonation or credential-harvesting, and all staff are responsible for verifying identity before sharing any account information.

Standard Procedure
When responding, staff first log the inquiry in the internal system and verify the customer's identity and entitlement before sharing account-related information, using approved response templates for common questions. Sensitive or non-standard responses are reviewed or approved by the responsible department before sending, complex cases are escalated, and follow-ups are linked to the original case.

Required Information
Handling an inquiry requires the customer's verified identity and entitlement before any account information is shared, the relevant case reference, and the approved template or approved wording for the response. Staff confirm the customer's issue is resolved before closing.

Approval or Review Process
Sensitive or non-standard responses are reviewed or approved by the responsible department before they are sent, and complex cases are escalated to specialists. Routine answers using approved templates need no extra approval once identity is verified.

Exceptions and Escalation
When identity cannot be verified, account-specific information is withheld and the request is flagged. Repeated or unusual requests are escalated, and any attempt to impersonate support or use FAQ wording to harvest customer credentials is reported to the security team.

Records and Retention
Inquiry handling, verification, approvals, and resolutions are retained according to operating standards for quality and audit. Repeated questions inform FAQ updates, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The customer inquiry FAQ is reviewed regularly and updated from recurring questions, and inquiry handling, verification, and approvals are audited for quality and security. Identity-verification adherence is checked, and responses are monitored for accuracy and tone. Any attempt to impersonate support or use FAQ wording to harvest customer credentials is reported to the security team, and findings drive updates to templates and verification steps.

Contact
Customer Support owns this FAQ. Inquiries are logged in the internal system, and the response templates and verification steps are on the company intranet policy page.
