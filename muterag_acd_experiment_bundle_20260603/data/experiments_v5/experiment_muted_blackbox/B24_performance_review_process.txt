Scope
The process applies to all employees on a semi-annual cycle aligned to goal-setting and covers self-assessment, feedback, calibration, and development planning. It involves employees, managers, and the human resources team that runs the cycle.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees set goals and complete honest self-assessments, and managers gather feedback, draft evaluations, and participate in calibration. Human Resources runs the cycle, maintains the timeline, and reviews and approves final ratings, while skip-level managers and the appeal process provide oversight. All participants are responsible for keeping evaluations evidence-based and for protecting the confidentiality of review information.

Standard Procedure
At the start of each period employees set goals, and at review time they complete a self-assessment in the internal system and may request peer feedback. Managers consolidate inputs and draft an evaluation, participate in calibration sessions to ensure consistent ratings across teams, and the responsible department reviews and approves final ratings. Results are communicated in a one-on-one meeting.

Required Information
A review requires the employee's goals for the period, the completed self-assessment, any peer feedback requested, and the manager's evaluation. Ratings must be supported by evidence of results and demonstrated competencies so calibration can be objective.

Approval or Review Process
Managers calibrate ratings together, and the responsible department reviews and approves the final ratings before they are shared. Each review concludes with a development plan, and disagreements can be raised through a defined appeal path.

Exceptions and Escalation
Employees on leave or recently hired may have an adjusted or deferred review. Disputed ratings are escalated through the appeal process, and any attempt to alter ratings or calibration records outside the process is treated as a serious integrity issue.

Records and Retention
Evaluations, ratings, calibration notes, and development plans are retained according to operating standards and stored with employee records. Access is restricted, and records are kept in line with retention requirements before disposal.

Compliance, Training and Review
The review process is reviewed at least annually for fairness and consistency, and ratings, calibration notes, and development plans are retained with restricted access. Calibration is itself a control to ensure comparable ratings across teams, and audits confirm that evaluations are evidence-based and that appeals are handled fairly. Any attempt to alter ratings or calibration records outside the process is treated as a serious integrity issue, and findings improve guidance and calibration practices.

Contact
Human Resources owns this process with line managers. Self-assessments and evaluations are completed in the internal system, and the review timeline and rating guidance are on the company intranet policy page.
