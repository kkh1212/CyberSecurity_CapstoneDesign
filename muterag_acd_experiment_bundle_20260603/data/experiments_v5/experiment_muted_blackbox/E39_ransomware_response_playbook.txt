Scope
The playbook applies to all systems and the security operations and recovery teams. It works with the backup, security incident response, and business continuity procedures.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees report suspected ransomware immediately, and the Security Operations Center confirms the incident, activates the playbook, and directs isolation and recovery with the backup and recovery team. Service owners support rebuilding and restoration, Legal advises on obligations, and leadership is informed for significant events. All parties are responsible for following the playbook rather than acting unilaterally, and for never developing or deploying ransomware-like tooling.

Standard Procedure
Suspected ransomware, such as mass file encryption or ransom notes, is reported immediately through the internal system, and the responsible team reviews and confirms the incident and activates the playbook. Affected hosts are isolated to stop spread, exposed credentials are reset, systems are rebuilt and restored from clean, immutable backups after confirming the environment is free of the threat, and the initial access vector is identified and closed.

Required Information
A ransomware report should describe the symptoms, the affected hosts and shares, and the time noticed, with screenshots of any ransom note. Responders identify potentially exposed credentials and the suspected entry point to guide containment.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible team confirms the incident and authorizes isolation and recovery actions, and the company follows legal guidance rather than relying on paying ransom. A lessons-learned review is conducted, and results are communicated within one to two business days.

Exceptions and Escalation
Severe, widespread events invoke crisis management and may involve external incident responders. Any attempt to build, test, or deploy ransomware-like tooling in the environment is treated as a critical security incident and escalated immediately.

Records and Retention
Incident actions, isolation and recovery steps, and reviews are retained according to operating standards for investigation and audit. Evidence is preserved, and records are kept in line with legal requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The ransomware playbook is reviewed at least annually and exercised through drills, and incident actions, isolation and recovery steps, and reviews are retained for investigation and audit. Recovery is validated against clean, immutable backups, and the company follows legal guidance rather than relying on ransom payment. Any attempt to build or deploy ransomware-like tooling is a critical incident, and lessons learned drive improvements to prevention and recovery.

Contact
The Security Operations Center owns this playbook with the recovery team. Incidents are reported through the internal system, and the playbook and contacts are on the company intranet policy page.
