Scope
The policy covers all user and administrative accounts on corporate systems, cloud services, and applications integrated with the company identity provider. It applies to every employee and contractor and to service accounts where interactive login is possible. Personal accounts used for work are out of scope but must never reuse corporate passwords.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees keep their credentials confidential, enroll the required MFA methods, and report lost devices immediately. Managers confirm identity for sensitive resets and bypass requests. Identity Operations and the IT help desk administer enrollment, resets, and lockout recovery, and the security team monitors authentication events for abuse. Administrators and finance staff carry additional obligations because hardware keys are mandatory for their accounts, and application owners ensure their systems honor the central authentication and MFA requirements rather than maintaining separate password stores.

Standard Procedure
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and combine upper- and lower-case letters, digits, and symbols, and reuse of the previous five passwords is blocked. All employees enroll at least one MFA method, a time-based one-time password app or a FIDO2 hardware security key, with hardware keys mandatory for administrators and finance staff. To register or reset an MFA device, the employee submits a request through the internal identity portal.

Required Information
A device registration or reset request should include the employee identifier, the device type being enrolled, and the reason for the change. For resets, recent successful login context or manager confirmation may be requested to confirm identity. Lost or stolen authentication devices must be reported with the approximate time of loss so they can be revoked quickly.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible IT administrator reviews and approves device registrations and resets, and temporary bypass codes are issued only after manager confirmation. Privileged-account password changes follow a 90-day cycle. Results are communicated within one to two business days, and high-risk resets may require an additional identity check at the IT help desk.

Exceptions and Escalation
If an employee cannot use a standard MFA method, an approved alternative such as a backup hardware key may be granted with a documented justification. Five consecutive failed logins lock the account for thirty minutes, and repeated lockouts or suspected compromise are escalated to the security team for investigation and possible forced reset.

Records and Retention
Authentication events, device enrollments, reset approvals, and lockout incidents are retained according to operating standards and feed the central security monitoring system. Records support investigations into suspicious access and are kept in line with audit requirements before secure disposal.

Compliance, Training and Review
New joiners enroll MFA during onboarding, and periodic checks confirm that all active accounts have a valid second factor. The policy is reviewed at least annually and whenever authentication technology or threats change materially. Authentication and lockout records feed central monitoring, and repeated lockouts or anomalous logins trigger investigation. Accounts without compliant MFA may be restricted until remediated, and audit reviews confirm that privileged-account rotation and hardware-key requirements are being met across the organization.

Contact
Identity Operations and the IT help desk manage credential and MFA support. Employees can request help through the internal identity portal, and the current MFA enrollment guide and recovery instructions are available on the company intranet policy page.
