Scope
The policy applies to all company endpoints and the endpoint operations team that manages them. Non-compliant endpoints lose access to sensitive resources, linking this policy to the remote access and network access control standards.

Roles and Responsibilities
Endpoint Operations maintains the compliance baseline, patch schedule, and remediation tooling, and reviews and approves exceptions with the security team. Employees keep their endpoints enrolled and compliant, and application owners flag where patches would break critical software. The security team monitors compliance and EDR health, and all users are responsible for not disabling security agents or falsifying compliance status.

Standard Procedure
All endpoints are enrolled in endpoint management and must meet a compliance baseline including disk encryption, an active EDR agent, and a supported operating system. Security patches are deployed on a risk-based schedule, with critical vulnerabilities patched within a defined emergency window, and patch deployment is staged and monitored for failures. Endpoints that fall out of compliance are automatically remediated or quarantined.

Required Information
A patch or configuration exception request should identify the endpoint or group, the control that cannot be met, the business reason, the compensating controls, and the remediation date. Standard compliance requires no request, only enrollment and adherence to the baseline.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves exceptions with an expiry date and compensating controls. Critical patches follow the emergency window, and persistent compliance failures are escalated for remediation or quarantine.

Exceptions and Escalation
Time-bound exceptions are available where patching would break a critical application, provided compensating controls are in place. Lost or compromised devices are remotely locked and wiped, and any attempt to disable the EDR agent or falsify compliance status is treated as a security incident.

Records and Retention
Compliance status, patch results, and exceptions are retained according to operating standards for audit and reporting. The baseline definition is maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The endpoint and patch policy is reviewed at least annually, and compliance status, patch results, and exceptions are audited to confirm timely patching and a healthy baseline. Critical patches are tracked against the emergency window, and non-compliant endpoints lose access to sensitive resources until remediated. Disabling the EDR agent or falsifying compliance is treated as a security incident, and findings refine the baseline and patch SLAs.

Contact
Endpoint Operations owns this policy with the security team. Exception requests are submitted through the internal system, and the baseline definition and patch schedule are on the company intranet policy page.
