Scope
The policy covers logs from systems, applications, and network devices collected centrally in the SIEM, and applies to the security operations team and system owners. It supports the incident response and audit processes.

Roles and Responsibilities
System owners forward security-relevant logs to the central SIEM, and the Security Operations Center maintains collection, retention, and tamper protection and reviews access and retention requests. Investigators justify access to raw logs, and the privacy and legal teams advise on legal holds. All parties are responsible for not deleting, altering, or disabling logging, since log integrity underpins detection and investigation.

Standard Procedure
Security-relevant logs are collected centrally in the SIEM and include authentication, administrative actions, and security events. Logs are retained for defined periods based on type and legal requirements, with security logs kept for at least one year, and they are write-once where possible and protected from modification. Requests to access raw logs or extend retention are submitted through the internal system and reviewed and approved by the responsible department.

Required Information
A log-access or retention-change request should specify the systems, the time range, the purpose, and the requester's role. Access to raw logs is restricted and itself logged, so requests justify the need for direct access rather than standard dashboards.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves log access and retention changes, and periodic reviews confirm coverage and integrity. Time synchronization ensures consistent timestamps across sources for reliable correlation.

Exceptions and Escalation
Legal holds may extend retention beyond standard periods, and incident investigations may grant temporary broader access under approval. Any attempt to delete, alter, or disable logging is treated as a serious security incident and escalated immediately.

Records and Retention
Retention settings, access approvals, and integrity checks are retained according to operating standards for audit. The logging standard and coverage map are maintained centrally, and records are kept in line with compliance obligations.

Compliance, Training and Review
The log retention and audit policy is reviewed at least annually, and retention settings, access approvals, and integrity checks are audited to confirm coverage and tamper resistance. Security logs are kept for at least one year, time synchronization is verified, and access to raw logs is itself logged. Any attempt to delete, alter, or disable logging is treated as a serious incident, and findings drive improvements to coverage and protection.

Contact
The Security Operations Center owns this policy. Log-access and retention requests are submitted through the internal system, and the logging standard is on the company intranet policy page.
