Scope
The procedure covers USB drives, external hard disks, memory cards, and any other removable storage connected to company endpoints. It applies to all employees and contractors using managed devices; by default, USB mass-storage ports are disabled by endpoint management until an approved, registered device is used.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees request, register, and safeguard removable media and report loss immediately. The responsible department approves requests and confirms encryption and classification limits, and Endpoint Security maintains port controls, the registered-device list, and transfer logging. The IT help desk performs secure wiping on return, and data owners are consulted when Confidential data is involved so that the relevant export approval is in place before any transfer to removable media.

Standard Procedure
To use removable media, an employee submits a request through the internal system describing the device and the business need, and the responsible department reviews and approves it. Approved devices are registered to the user and must be hardware-encrypted, and only registered, encrypted devices may be mounted. All file transfers to removable media are logged by DLP.

Required Information
A removable-media request should include the device model and serial where available, the data to be transferred and its classification, the destination or recipient, and the duration of need. Requests involving Confidential data must reference the relevant export approval, and personal storage devices are never permitted for company data.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department confirms the business need, the classification limits, and the encryption requirement before approving, and results are communicated within one to two business days. Approvals can be time-bound, and high-sensitivity transfers may require additional security sign-off.

Exceptions and Escalation
If encryption or registration cannot be met for an urgent, legitimate need, a temporary exception with compensating controls may be granted and logged. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately for remote revocation, and suspected misuse is escalated to the security team as an incident.

Records and Retention
Device registrations, transfer logs, approvals, and decommissioning records are retained according to operating standards. When a device is no longer needed it is returned to IT for secure wiping, and the wipe is recorded. Retention aligns with audit and legal requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
Removable-media rules are included in security awareness training, and the procedure is reviewed at least annually. Device registrations, transfer logs, and decommissioning records are audited to confirm that only registered, encrypted devices are used and that returns are wiped. Unusual transfer volumes are reviewed, and lost-device reports are tracked to revocation. Attempts to use unregistered media or bypass logging are escalated as incidents, and audit findings drive adjustments to port-control and encryption enforcement.

Contact
Endpoint Security owns this procedure with support from the IT help desk. Employees submit device requests through the internal system, and the request form and the list of approved encrypted devices are on the company intranet policy page.
