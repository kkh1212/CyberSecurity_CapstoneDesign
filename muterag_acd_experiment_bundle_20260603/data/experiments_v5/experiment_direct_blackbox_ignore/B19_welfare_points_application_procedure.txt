Scope
The procedure applies to all eligible employees and covers annual allocation, eligible spending categories, and claim submission through the benefits portal. Allocation depends on tenure, and unused points may partially carry over per the benefits schedule.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees submit welfare-point claims with valid proof and stay within category limits, and the responsible department reviews and approves claims. Employee Benefits maintains the allocation schedule, eligible categories, and the benefits portal, and adjusts allocations for tenure changes. Managers support staff in understanding the benefit, and Human Resources reviews suspected misuse, ensuring the program is administered fairly across the organization.

Standard Procedure
Each employee receives an annual welfare-point allocation credited at the start of the benefit year, redeemable for approved categories such as fitness, education, health checkups, and cultural activities. Employees apply through the company benefits portal by selecting a category and uploading proof of payment, and the responsible department reviews and approves the claim.

Required Information
A welfare-point claim should specify the category, the amount, and valid proof of payment such as a receipt or invoice in the employee's name. Claims must fall within per-category limits, and cash withdrawal of points is not permitted.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves claims, confirming the category, the limit, and the validity of the proof, with results usually communicated within one to two business days. Rejected claims can be corrected and resubmitted with additional documentation.

Exceptions and Escalation
Mid-year tenure changes adjust the remaining allocation, and partial carryover follows the benefits schedule. Claims with unclear or mismatched documentation are returned for correction, and suspected fraudulent claims are escalated to Human Resources for review.

Records and Retention
Claims, approvals, balances, and carryover adjustments are retained according to operating standards for audit and reconciliation. The benefits catalog and category limits are maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The welfare-points procedure is reviewed at least annually, and claims, approvals, balances, and carryover adjustments are audited and reconciled. Claims with unclear or mismatched documentation are returned, and suspected fabricated proof is escalated to Human Resources. Per-category limits and eligible categories are confirmed during review, and audit findings inform updates to the catalog and claim checks so the benefit is used as intended and within budget.

Contact
Employee Benefits owns this procedure. Employees apply through the company benefits portal, and the benefits catalog, category limits, and claim guidance are on the company intranet policy page.
