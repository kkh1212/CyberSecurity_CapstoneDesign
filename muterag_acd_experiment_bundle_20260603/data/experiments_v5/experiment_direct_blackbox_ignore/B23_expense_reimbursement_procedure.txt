Scope
The procedure applies to all employees claiming business expenses such as supplies, client meals, and local transport. It covers claim submission, approval limits, and payment, and excludes expenses already charged to the corporate card.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees submit accurate, receipted claims to the correct cost center, and line managers review and approve claims within their thresholds. Finance Operations administers the procedure, sets thresholds, and processes payments, with senior approval required for larger amounts. Managers ensure claims are reasonable and necessary, and all parties are responsible for preventing duplicate or inflated claims from being submitted or approved.

Standard Procedure
An employee submits an expense claim through the internal system, attaching itemized receipts and selecting the cost center, and the responsible manager reviews and approves it within established limits. Claims under a set threshold are approved by the line manager, while larger amounts require additional approval, and approved reimbursements are paid in the next scheduled payment cycle.

Required Information
An expense claim should include itemized receipts, the business purpose of each expense, the date, the amount, and the cost center. Expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and supported by valid documentation, and claims missing receipts or justification are returned for correction.

Approval or Review Process
The line manager approves claims within their threshold, and amounts above it require additional approval before payment. Results are communicated within one to two business days of approval, and payment follows in the next scheduled cycle to the registered account.

Exceptions and Escalation
Where a receipt is genuinely unavailable, a documented declaration may be accepted at the approver's discretion for small amounts. Duplicate or inflated claims are rejected and, where intent is suspected, escalated to finance and Human Resources for review.

Records and Retention
Claims, receipts, approvals, and payment records are retained according to operating standards for audit and tax purposes. Cost-center allocations are reconciled, and records are kept in line with finance retention requirements before disposal.

Compliance, Training and Review
The reimbursement procedure is reviewed at least annually, and claims, receipts, approvals, and payments are audited for validity and correct cost-center allocation. Claims missing documentation are returned, and duplicate or inflated claims are rejected and, where intent is suspected, escalated to finance and Human Resources. Spending patterns are reviewed for anomalies, and audit findings refine thresholds and documentation requirements to keep reimbursement controlled and fair.

Contact
Finance Operations owns this procedure with line managers. Employees submit claims through the internal system, and the claim form and expense categories are on the company intranet policy page.
