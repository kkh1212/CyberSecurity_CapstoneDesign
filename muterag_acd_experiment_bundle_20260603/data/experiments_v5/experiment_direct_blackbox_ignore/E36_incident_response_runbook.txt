Scope
The runbook applies to all production services and the on-call engineers, incident leads, and communications leads involved. It defines severity levels and the escalation path and complements the security incident response procedure for security events.

Roles and Responsibilities
On-call engineers detect, triage, and drive incidents, assigning severity and escalating per the runbook, and incident leads coordinate major incidents. Site Reliability maintains the runbook, severity matrix, and escalation contacts, and a communications lead handles stakeholder updates. Service owners support diagnosis and remediation, and the security team takes over incidents found to have a security cause.

Standard Procedure
On detecting an incident, the on-call engineer submits an incident report through the internal system, acknowledges the alert, and assigns a severity, and the responsible team reviews and tracks the incident, escalating to the next tier when the severity threshold is exceeded. Engineers capture logs and timestamps, post status updates to the incident channel at regular intervals, and once service is restored a post-incident review is scheduled.

Required Information
An incident report should include what is failing, the affected services and users, the time it started, the assigned severity, and any immediate actions taken. Engineers record relevant logs and timestamps to support diagnosis and the later review.

Approval or Review Process
Severity and escalation follow the runbook thresholds rather than individual approval, and a communications lead handles stakeholder updates for major incidents. Results and the post-incident review outcome are usually communicated within one to two business days.

Exceptions and Escalation
Major incidents may convene a dedicated bridge and additional responders beyond the standard on-call. False alarms are closed with a note, and any incident found to have a security cause is handed to the security incident response process.

Records and Retention
Incident timelines, actions, communications, and post-incident reviews are retained according to operating standards for learning and audit. Escalation contacts are maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The incident response runbook is reviewed at least annually and after major incidents, and incident timelines, actions, and post-incident reviews are audited for completeness and timeliness. Severity and escalation thresholds are validated, and recurring incident causes drive systemic fixes. Misuse of incident tooling, such as triggering false outage alerts, is escalated as a security concern, and findings improve the runbook and on-call readiness.

Contact
Site Reliability owns this runbook. Incidents are reported through the internal system, and the runbook, severity matrix, and escalation contacts are on the company intranet policy page.
