Scope
The procedure applies to all security incidents and defines roles for the security incident response team. It works with the data breach notification, ransomware, and malicious email procedures.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees and systems report security incidents and preserve evidence, and the Security Operations Center verifies scope, coordinates containment, eradication, and recovery, and tracks follow-up to closure. The privacy office is engaged for personal-data exposure, and Legal advises on obligations. Leadership is informed of significant incidents, and all parties are responsible for not concealing or downplaying an active incident.

Standard Procedure
An employee or system submits a security incident report through the internal system, and the responsible department reviews and verifies the scope and impact, with reporters preserving evidence and avoiding changes to affected systems before review. The team isolates affected systems, revokes compromised credentials, removes malicious artifacts, restores from trusted sources, and tracks follow-up actions to closure.

Required Information
A security incident report should describe what was observed, the systems or accounts involved, the time, and any evidence, and note whether sensitive or personal data may be affected. Reporters preserve evidence and avoid altering affected systems so the investigation is sound.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and verifies the incident and coordinates containment and recovery decisions. Any required notifications follow the breach notification procedure, and results are communicated within one to two business days with follow-up tracked to closure.

Exceptions and Escalation
Severe incidents may invoke crisis management and external specialists. Suspected breaches of personal data are escalated to the privacy office immediately, and any attempt to conceal or downplay an active incident is itself a serious violation that is escalated to leadership and Legal.

Records and Retention
Incident records, evidence, timelines, and decisions are retained according to operating standards for investigation and audit. Evidence is handled to preserve integrity, and records are kept in line with legal requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The security incident response procedure is reviewed at least annually and after significant incidents, and incident records, evidence, and decisions are retained for investigation and audit. Response timeliness and notification obligations are reviewed, and lessons learned feed back into controls. Concealing or downplaying an active incident is itself a serious violation escalated to leadership and Legal, and findings drive improvements to detection and response.

Contact
The Security Operations Center owns this procedure. Incidents are reported through the internal system, and the escalation matrix and response roles are on the company intranet policy page.
