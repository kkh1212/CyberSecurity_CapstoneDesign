Scope
The manual covers account creation for employees, contractors, and temporary staff across corporate systems and integrated applications. It applies to hiring managers, the identity operations team, and resource owners, and provisioning is triggered only by a confirmed hire record from Human Resources.

Roles and Responsibilities
Hiring managers initiate provisioning and confirm the role profile, and Human Resources owns the authoritative hire record that triggers the process. Identity Operations creates and verifies accounts and applies least-privilege baselines, while resource owners approve any elevated entitlements. The new hire is responsible for enrolling MFA and changing the initial password at first login, and the IT help desk supports setup issues during onboarding.

Standard Procedure
A hiring manager submits a new account request through the internal system specifying the role, team, and start date, and the responsible department reviews and approves it. The new hire's identity is verified before any credentials are issued, accounts are provisioned with a baseline role following least privilege, and additional entitlements require separate access requests. Credentials are delivered securely and MFA enrolled at first login.

Required Information
A provisioning request should include the new hire's name, role, team, manager, start date, and the standard role profile to apply. Any non-standard access must be requested separately with justification, and the identity verification step requires confirmation that the hire record is genuine and active.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves provisioning, confirming the role profile and the verified hire record before credentials are issued. Baseline access is granted by default, while elevated entitlements follow the access request and approval procedure with the relevant resource owners.

Exceptions and Escalation
Urgent same-day starts may use an expedited path with manager attestation and a follow-up verification. Discrepancies in the hire record, or requests that cannot be tied to a confirmed hire, are paused and escalated to Human Resources and the identity team before any account is created.

Records and Retention
Account creation, identity verification, and approval records are retained according to operating standards for audit and to support periodic access reviews. Initial credential delivery and first-login enrollment are logged, and records are kept in line with retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
Provisioning practices are reviewed at least annually, and account creation and verification records feed periodic access reviews that detect orphaned or over-privileged accounts. Audits confirm that accounts trace to verified hire records and that baselines follow least privilege. Discrepancies are escalated to Human Resources and the identity team, and findings drive improvements to role profiles and the verification step so that no account is created without a confirmed, active hire.

Contact
Identity Operations owns this manual with support from the IT help desk. Managers submit requests through the internal system, and the provisioning checklist and role-profile catalog are published on the company intranet policy page.
