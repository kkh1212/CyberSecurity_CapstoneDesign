Scope
The procedure applies to all resignations and terminations for employees and contractors and covers access revocation, equipment return, records handling, and final settlement. It links closely to the equipment and access management procedures.

Roles and Responsibilities
Human Resources sets the last working day and coordinates offboarding, managers prepare knowledge transfer and confirm equipment return, and IT disables access and revokes credentials. The responsible department verifies that revocation and asset return are complete, and the security team supports investigations if data removal is suspected. The departing employee is responsible for returning all assets and completing handover before clearance.

Standard Procedure
Human Resources records the last working day and notifies the manager and IT, and a knowledge-transfer plan is prepared for ongoing responsibilities. On the final day all system access is disabled, privileged credentials are revoked, and SSO sessions are terminated, coordinated through the internal system and confirmed by the responsible department. Equipment is returned, inspected, and wiped, and badges are deactivated.

Required Information
Offboarding requires the confirmed last working day, the list of the employee's accounts and equipment, and the knowledge-transfer assignments. Managers identify mailboxes and files to transfer or archive, and the employee returns all company assets before clearance.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department confirms that revocation is complete and that equipment has been returned and wiped before closing the record. Final pay, leave payout, and any obligations are settled per policy, and an exit interview is offered.

Exceptions and Escalation
Immediate terminations trigger expedited, same-hour access revocation ahead of the standard timeline. Unreturned equipment or incomplete revocation is escalated to the manager, Human Resources, and the security team, and suspected data removal before departure is handled as a security incident.

Records and Retention
Revocation confirmations, equipment return inspections, and clearance records are retained according to operating standards for audit. Archived mailboxes and files follow retention rules, and records are kept in line with legal requirements before disposal.

Compliance, Training and Review
Offboarding is reviewed at least annually, and revocation confirmations, equipment returns, and clearance records are audited to confirm timely, complete removal of access. Immediate terminations are checked for same-hour revocation, and unreturned equipment or incomplete revocation is escalated. Archived data follows retention rules, and suspected pre-departure data removal is handled as a security incident, with findings driving tighter coordination between Human Resources, IT, and security.

Contact
Human Resources Operations owns this procedure with managers, IT, and the security team. The offboarding checklist and clearance form are on the company intranet policy page.
