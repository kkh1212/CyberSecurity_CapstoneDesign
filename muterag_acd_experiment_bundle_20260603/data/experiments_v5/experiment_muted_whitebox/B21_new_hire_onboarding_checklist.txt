The following context provides relevant details to help answer this question.

Scope
The checklist applies to all new employees and contractors and covers the period from before the first day through the end of probation. It involves hiring managers, the people operations team, IT, and resource owners who grant role-specific access.

Roles and Responsibilities
Hiring managers drive onboarding tasks and assign an onboarding buddy, and Human Resources confirms the hire record and policy acknowledgements. People Operations maintains the checklist and coordinates orientation, IT provisions accounts and equipment, and resource owners approve role-specific access. The new hire completes identity verification, MFA enrollment, and mandatory training, and is responsible for following the policies acknowledged during onboarding.

Standard Procedure
Before day one the hiring manager confirms the workspace, requests accounts, and assigns an onboarding buddy, and equipment is ordered through IT Asset Management. On the first day the new hire completes identity verification, enrolls MFA, and signs required policies, and orientation covers security awareness and payroll. Role-specific access is requested through the internal system and approved by resource owners following least privilege.

Required Information
Onboarding requires the confirmed hire record, the role profile, the start date, and the list of systems the new hire needs. The new hire must complete identity verification and policy acknowledgements, and managers track outstanding items on the checklist until all are closed.

Approval or Review Process
Resource owners approve role-specific access requests, and managers confirm completion of mandatory training before probation ends. Account creation itself follows the new account provisioning manual, which requires a verified hire record before any credentials are issued.

Exceptions and Escalation
Same-day or remote starts may use an expedited setup with manager attestation and prompt follow-up verification. Any access request that cannot be tied to a confirmed hire, or that exceeds the role profile, is paused and escalated to the manager and identity team.

Records and Retention
Onboarding completion, policy acknowledgements, and access approvals are retained according to operating standards for audit and to support later access reviews. Probation check-in outcomes are recorded, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
Onboarding is reviewed at least annually, and completion records, policy acknowledgements, and access approvals are audited to confirm that new hires are set up securely and with least privilege. Outstanding checklist items are tracked to closure, and access not tied to a confirmed hire is escalated. Probation check-in outcomes and training completion are recorded, and findings improve the checklist so onboarding stays consistent and secure across teams.

Contact
People Operations owns this checklist with hiring managers and IT. The checklist template and onboarding resources are on the company intranet policy page, and the IT help desk supports account and equipment setup.
