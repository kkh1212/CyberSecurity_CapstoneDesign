Scope
The procedure applies to all entitlements across corporate and cloud systems, for every employee and contractor. It works with the privileged access and access review procedures and relies on the resource owners who approve entitlements.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees request access with a business justification, and resource owners decide whether to grant each entitlement. The security team approves high-risk entitlements and reviews segregation-of-duties exceptions, and Access Management operates the request workflow and entitlement catalog. Managers support recertification of their team's access, and all parties are responsible for ensuring access follows least privilege and is removed when no longer needed.

Standard Procedure
An employee submits an access request through the internal system selecting the entitlement and the business reason, and the resource owner reviews and approves it; high-risk entitlements require an additional security approval. Conflicting entitlements are automatically flagged and blocked, temporary access is granted with an expiry date, and emergency access uses a break-glass workflow reviewed afterward.

Required Information
An access request should identify the entitlement, the system, the business justification, and the expected duration. For elevated or sensitive access, the requester notes any compensating controls, and requests that trigger a segregation-of-duties conflict must include an explanation if an exception is sought.

Approval or Review Process
Resource owners approve standard requests, and high-risk entitlements require security approval, with results communicated within one to two business days. Segregation-of-duties conflicts, such as creating and approving the same payment, are blocked unless a documented compensating control is approved.

Exceptions and Escalation
Break-glass emergency access is available for incidents and is reviewed after the fact, with any unjustified use revoked. Exceptions to segregation-of-duties rules require documented compensating controls and time-limited approval, and unused access is removed during periodic reviews.

Records and Retention
Approvals, justifications, expiry dates, and emergency-access reviews are retained according to operating standards to support audit and recertification. The entitlement catalog and conflict rules are maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
Access practices are reviewed at least annually, and approvals, justifications, and emergency-access usage are audited for appropriateness. Segregation-of-duties rules are tested to confirm conflicting entitlements are blocked, and break-glass access is reviewed after every use. Unused or excessive access surfaced by periodic reviews is revoked, and audit findings refine the conflict rules and the entitlement catalog so that access stays aligned with roles over time.

Contact
Access Management owns this procedure with the resource owners and security team. Employees submit requests through the internal system, and the entitlement catalog and request guidance are on the company intranet policy page.
