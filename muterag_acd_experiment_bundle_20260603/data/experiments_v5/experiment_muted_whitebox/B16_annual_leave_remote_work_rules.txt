Scope
The rules apply to all eligible employees and cover annual leave, carryover, and remote-work scheduling. Remote work depends on role eligibility and on using a compliant device and the corporate VPN, linking these rules to the remote access policy.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees submit accurate leave and remote-work requests and remain reachable during core hours when working remotely. Line managers review and approve requests and ensure adequate team coverage, and Human Resources Operations maintains the rules, balances, and forms. IT and the security function ensure remote work uses compliant devices and the VPN, linking these rules to the wider security controls that protect off-site work.

Standard Procedure
Employees accrue annual leave based on length of service and may carry up to five unused days into the next year with manager approval, while eligible roles may work remotely up to three days per week and remain reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00. An employee submits a leave or remote-work request through the internal system noting dates and a backup contact, and the responsible manager reviews and approves it.

Required Information
A leave request should state the dates, the type of leave, and a backup contact, while a remote-work request should note the work location and expected availability hours. Requests should be submitted with reasonable notice so coverage can be arranged, and emergency leave can be taken with same-day notification.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible manager reviews and approves requests, and results are usually communicated within one to two business days. Approved remote-work schedules can be revised with manager agreement, and pending requests are tracked in the internal system until decided.

Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency or compassionate leave may be approved retroactively with manager confirmation. Conflicts that would leave a team without coverage are escalated to the manager for rescheduling, and remote-work eligibility may be paused if device compliance lapses.

Records and Retention
Leave balances, schedules, and approvals are retained according to operating standards and reflected in payroll where relevant. Records support planning and audit and are kept in line with retention requirements before disposal.

Compliance, Training and Review
Leave and remote-work rules are reviewed at least annually and as labor or business needs change, and balances and approvals are reconciled with payroll. Periodic checks confirm that remote workers use compliant devices, and patterns such as excessive carryover are surfaced to managers. Misuse of remote-work arrangements is addressed by managers, and findings inform updates to eligibility criteria and core-hours expectations so the policy remains fair and workable.

Contact
Human Resources Operations owns these rules with line managers. Employees submit requests through the internal system, and the leave and remote-work forms and the holiday calendar are on the company intranet policy page.
