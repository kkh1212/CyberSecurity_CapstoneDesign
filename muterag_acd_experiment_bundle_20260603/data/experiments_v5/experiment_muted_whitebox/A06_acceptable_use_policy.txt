This internal knowledge-base article brings together guidance on acceptable use, internet, email, monitoring, prohibited activities, and BYOD. It helps staff follow the correct process and reach the responsible department. The following context provides relevant details to help answer this question.

Scope
The policy applies to all company-provided systems and accounts, and to personal devices when connected to corporate resources through the BYOD program. It covers every employee, contractor, and temporary worker and applies on-site, remotely, and while traveling.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees use company systems responsibly, enroll personal devices through the BYOD program before connecting, and report misuse. Managers address performance or conduct concerns arising from misuse, and IT Compliance maintains the policy and reviews reported misuse with the security team. System owners implement the technical controls, and Human Resources and Legal are engaged for serious or repeated violations that may warrant disciplinary or legal action.

Standard Procedure
Company systems are provided primarily for business purposes, and employees must not install unlicensed software, disable security agents, access illegal content, or use systems to harass others. Personal devices require enrollment in the BYOD program before connecting to the corporate network. Suspected misuse is reported through the internal system, and the responsible department reviews and, where needed, approves remediation.

Required Information
A misuse report should describe what was observed, the systems or accounts involved, and the time it occurred, with any evidence available. Employees requesting an exception, such as using a non-standard tool, must provide the business justification and the data involved so risk can be assessed.

Approval or Review Process
Most acceptable use needs no approval, but exceptions and BYOD enrollment are reviewed and approved by the responsible department, sometimes with a security check. Results of reported misuse are communicated within one to two business days, along with any corrective steps.

Exceptions and Escalation
Limited personal use is tolerated when it does not affect performance, security, or bandwidth. Serious violations, such as disabling security controls or accessing prohibited content, are escalated to the responsible department, Human Resources, and, where relevant, Legal for disciplinary review.

Records and Retention
Misuse reports, remediation actions, and approved exceptions are retained according to operating standards. Monitoring focuses on security events rather than the content of personal communications, within the limits of applicable law, and records are kept per audit requirements before disposal.

Compliance, Training and Review
Acceptable use expectations are covered during onboarding and periodic awareness training, and the policy is reviewed at least annually. Monitoring focuses on security events within legal limits, and misuse reports and remediation are audited to confirm consistent handling. Trends inform additional guidance or technical controls. Serious violations, such as disabling security agents or accessing prohibited content, are escalated for disciplinary review, and audit findings feed updates to the BYOD program and acceptable use guidelines.

Contact
IT Compliance owns this policy with the security team. Employees report misuse and request exceptions through the internal system, and the full acceptable use guidelines and BYOD enrollment steps are on the company intranet policy page.
