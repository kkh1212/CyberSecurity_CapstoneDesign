Scope
The plan applies to all critical business functions and the continuity team, with recovery objectives set per service based on a business impact analysis. It works with the backup and disaster recovery procedures.

Roles and Responsibilities
Business owners define critical functions and recovery objectives via the business impact analysis, and the Resilience Office maintains the plan and coordinates drills. The continuity team executes recovery in predefined roles, and IT operations performs failover and restoration. Leadership oversees major activations, and all teams are responsible for keeping recovery procedures current and for never sabotaging failover or recovery mechanisms.

Standard Procedure
A disruption is reported through the internal system, and the responsible department reviews and activates the plan, convening the continuity team with predefined roles for coordination, communication, and recovery. Critical systems use redundancy and documented failover procedures to meet recovery time and point objectives, and alternate work arrangements support staff during disruption.

Required Information
An activation report should describe the disruption, the affected functions, and the estimated impact. The continuity team references the business impact analysis and recovery objectives to prioritize restoration in the correct order.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and activates the plan, and the continuity team coordinates recovery against the defined objectives. The plan is tested through periodic drills and tabletop exercises, and gaps are remediated.

Exceptions and Escalation
Localized disruptions may invoke only the relevant portions of the plan rather than full activation. Drill failures or any deliberate sabotage of failover or recovery mechanisms are escalated immediately to leadership and the security team.

Records and Retention
Activation records, drill results, and plan versions are retained according to operating standards for audit and improvement. Recovery objectives are reviewed after each test, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The continuity and disaster recovery plan is reviewed at least annually and tested through periodic drills and tabletop exercises, with activation records, drill results, and plan versions retained for audit. Recovery objectives are validated after each test, and gaps are remediated. Drill failures or deliberate sabotage of failover or recovery mechanisms are escalated to leadership and the security team, and findings drive updates to objectives and procedures.

Contact
The Resilience Office owns this plan. Disruptions are reported through the internal system, and the continuity plan and contact tree are on the company intranet policy page.
