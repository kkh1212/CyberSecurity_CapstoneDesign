Scope
The procedure applies to all vendors that access or process company data and to the third-party risk team and business sponsors. It covers due diligence, contracts, and ongoing monitoring.

Roles and Responsibilities
Business sponsors initiate vendor engagements and define data flows, and Third-Party Risk assesses posture, sets conditions, and approves engagements with the security team. Vendors complete questionnaires and provide evidence, Legal ensures appropriate contract clauses, and the privacy office advises on personal data. All parties are responsible for not sharing data with a vendor before approval or based on a misrepresented assessment.

Standard Procedure
A vendor assessment request is submitted through the internal system, and the responsible department reviews the vendor's security posture using questionnaires and evidence. Approval is required before data sharing begins, with risk-based conditions, and contracts include data protection, breach notification, and audit-right clauses appropriate to the risk. Vendors are reassessed periodically and after significant changes or incidents.

Required Information
An assessment request should identify the vendor, the data and systems involved, the business purpose, and the proposed data flows. The vendor provides a completed security questionnaire and supporting evidence such as certifications.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews the posture and approves the engagement before any data sharing, attaching risk-based conditions. High-risk vendors require stronger controls and references, and access is removed when the engagement ends.

Exceptions and Escalation
Provisional approval with strict conditions may be granted pending minor remediation, tracked to a deadline. Discovery of a misrepresented assessment or an untrusted vendor gaining access is escalated to security and the business sponsor for immediate review.

Records and Retention
Assessments, approvals, contracts, and reassessments are retained according to operating standards for audit. The vendor inventory and questionnaire are maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The vendor assessment procedure is reviewed at least annually, and assessments, approvals, contracts, and reassessments are audited to confirm due diligence before data sharing. Vendors are reassessed periodically and after significant changes or incidents, and access is removed when engagements end. A misrepresented assessment or untrusted vendor gaining access is escalated to security and the sponsor, and findings refine the questionnaire and risk criteria.

Contact
Third-Party Risk owns this procedure with the security team. Assessment requests are submitted through the internal system, and the vendor questionnaire and risk criteria are on the company intranet policy page.
