Scope
The procedure applies to all production deployments across services and the engineers, reviewers, and release managers involved. All deployments flow through the CI/CD pipeline with required quality gates.

Roles and Responsibilities
Engineers prepare deployment requests with test evidence and rollback plans, and reviewers and release managers approve releases, with a second approver required for high-risk changes. Release Engineering maintains the pipeline and quality gates, and the change management function governs windows and emergency changes. Service owners verify post-deploy health, and all parties are responsible for not bypassing the pipeline or its approval gates.

Standard Procedure
A deployment request is submitted through the internal system with the change summary, test evidence, and rollback plan, and the responsible department reviews and approves it. Standard releases occur during approved change windows, emergency changes use expedited approval with post-implementation review, and high-risk changes require a second approver. Each deployment includes a tested rollback plan and post-deploy verification.

Required Information
A deployment request should include the change summary, the test evidence, the rollback plan, the target environment, and the intended change window. High-risk changes identify the second approver, and emergency changes state the justification for bypassing the standard window.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves deployments, with high-risk changes requiring a second approver, and results communicated within one to two business days for scheduled releases. Failed deployments are rolled back using the tested plan and recorded.

Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency fixes may deploy outside change windows with expedited approval and a mandatory post-implementation review. Any attempt to deploy without the required approval or to bypass the pipeline gates is treated as a serious change-control violation and escalated.

Records and Retention
Deployment approvals, test evidence, change windows, and outcomes are retained according to operating standards for audit. The release calendar and rollback records are maintained, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The deployment procedure is reviewed at least annually, and approvals, test evidence, change windows, and outcomes are audited to confirm controlled, reversible releases. Emergency changes are checked for required post-implementation reviews, and rollback records are verified. Any deployment without required approval or that bypasses pipeline gates is treated as a serious change-control violation and escalated, with findings driving improvements to gates, windows, and rollback testing.

Contact
Release Engineering owns this procedure with the change management function. Deployment requests are submitted through the internal system, and the release calendar and pipeline standards are on the company intranet policy page.
