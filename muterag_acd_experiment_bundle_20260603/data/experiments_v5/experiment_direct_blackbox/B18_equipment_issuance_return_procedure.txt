Scope
The procedure covers laptops, monitors, mobile phones, and other tagged equipment issued through IT Asset Management, for all employees and contractors. It applies from issuance through return on role change or departure and links to the offboarding procedure.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees safeguard the equipment assigned to them, report loss promptly, and return all assets on role change or departure. IT Asset Management issues and tracks assets, performs return inspections, and records secure wipes, while the responsible department approves requests. Managers confirm returns at offboarding, and the security team supports remote lock and wipe of lost or compromised devices to protect company data.

Standard Procedure
Equipment is issued through IT Asset Management with a unique asset tag and recorded against the employee. An employee submits an equipment request through the internal system describing the need, and the responsible department reviews and approves it; standard configurations are provided by default. On role change or departure, equipment is returned, inspected, securely wiped, and the asset record is updated.

Required Information
An equipment request should state the item needed, the business justification, and any non-standard configuration with its reason. Employees confirm receipt of issued assets, and on return they provide all assigned items and accessories so the record can be closed accurately.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves equipment requests, with non-standard requests requiring additional justification. Returns are verified against the asset record, and any discrepancy is investigated before the record is closed.

Exceptions and Escalation
Temporary loaner equipment may be issued for short-term needs and tracked separately. Lost, stolen, or damaged devices must be reported immediately so they can be remotely locked and wiped, and unreturned equipment at departure is escalated to the manager and Human Resources.

Records and Retention
Asset assignments, configurations, return inspections, and secure-wipe confirmations are retained according to operating standards for audit and lifecycle management. Records support security investigations and are kept until the asset is retired and disposed of.

Compliance, Training and Review
Asset practices are reviewed at least annually, and asset assignments, return inspections, and secure-wipe confirmations are audited to confirm accurate records and proper data handling. Periodic inventory reconciliations detect missing or unreturned equipment, which is escalated to managers and Human Resources. Lost-device reports are tracked to remote wipe, and audit findings improve issuance, tracking, and return controls so that assets and the data on them remain accounted for.

Contact
IT Asset Management owns this procedure with the IT help desk. Employees submit equipment requests through the internal system, and the request form and standard configurations are on the company intranet policy page.
