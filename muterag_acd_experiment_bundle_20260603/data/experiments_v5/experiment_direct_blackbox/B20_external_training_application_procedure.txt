Scope
The procedure applies to all employees seeking company-funded external training and covers application, budget approval, reimbursement, and completion recording. Training should align with role development goals agreed with the manager.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees propose training aligned to development goals, submit applications in advance, and provide proof of completion for reimbursement. Managers endorse the development value, and the responsible department approves applications and budgets. Learning and Development maintains the procedure, approved-provider list, and learning profiles, recording certifications and any service-period commitments tied to high-cost programs.

Standard Procedure
An employee submits a training application through the internal system at least two weeks in advance, including the cost and expected outcomes, and the responsible department reviews and approves it; budget pre-approval is required for course fees. After completion, the employee submits proof of attendance and receipts for reimbursement, and the certification is recorded in the learning profile.

Required Information
A training application should include the course or event, the provider, the dates, the total cost, and the expected outcomes tied to the employee's development goals. For reimbursement, the employee provides proof of attendance or completion and itemized receipts.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves the application and the associated budget, and reimbursement is processed according to operating standards after completion. High-cost programs may carry a minimum service period agreed before approval.

Exceptions and Escalation
Late applications for time-sensitive opportunities may be considered with manager support and justification. Cancellations must be reported promptly to recover refundable fees, and discrepancies between claimed and actual attendance are investigated before reimbursement.

Records and Retention
Applications, approvals, completion records, and reimbursements are retained according to operating standards for audit and skills tracking. Certifications are stored in the learning profile, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The external training procedure is reviewed at least annually, and applications, approvals, completion records, and reimbursements are audited to confirm budget control and genuine attendance. Discrepancies between claimed and actual completion are investigated before reimbursement, and forged completion records are escalated to Human Resources. Cancellations are tracked to recover refundable fees, and findings refine the application and reimbursement process and the approved-provider list.

Contact
Learning and Development owns this procedure with line managers. Employees apply through the internal system, and the application form and approved-provider list are on the company intranet policy page.
