Scope
The guide applies to all customer-facing incident notices and the customer communications team, covering drafting, approval, and updates. It works with the incident response runbook for accurate status.

Roles and Responsibilities
Communications staff draft incident notices, and the responsible department reviews and approves them before publication, coordinating wording with the incident response team. Customer Communications maintains the templates and status-page process, and executives review high-visibility incidents. All staff are responsible for publishing only approved, accurate notices and never issuing fake or misleading outage notifications.

Standard Procedure
Notices state the affected service, impact, current status, and next update time, avoiding internal jargon and speculation, using severity-appropriate templates. Staff draft a notice and submit it through the internal system, the responsible department reviews and approves it before publishing to the status page, and updates follow at the promised cadence until resolution; a closing notice and, for major incidents, a summary are published afterward.

Required Information
A notice should include the affected service, the customer impact, the current status, and the next update time, written in clear external language. Wording is coordinated with the incident response team so it is accurate.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves each notice before it is published to the status page, and updates follow the promised cadence. Major-incident summaries are coordinated with the incident response team for accuracy.

Exceptions and Escalation
Severe, high-visibility incidents may require executive review before publication. Any publication of a fake or unapproved outage notice intended to mislead customers is a serious violation and escalated to leadership and the security team.

Records and Retention
Published notices, approvals, and update history are retained according to operating standards for audit and review. Templates are maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The incident notice guide is reviewed regularly, and published notices, approvals, and update history are audited for accuracy and timeliness. Update cadence against commitments is checked, and major-incident summaries are validated for consistency with the incident record. Publishing a fake or unapproved outage notice is a serious violation escalated to leadership and the security team, and findings refine templates and the approval process.

Contact
Customer Communications owns this guide. Notices are submitted through the internal system, and the notice templates and status-page process are on the company intranet policy page.
