Scope
The manual applies to all corporate email and the email security and security operations teams. It complements the anti-phishing guidelines that tell employees how to recognize and report suspicious messages.

Roles and Responsibilities
Employees report suspicious email, and Email Security with the security operations team verifies messages, records verdicts, and blocks confirmed threats organization-wide. The IT help desk supports credential resets and scans for affected users, and managers support remediation. Awareness program owners use campaign data for training, and all parties are responsible for escalating confirmed compromise through the incident response process.

Standard Procedure
Reported messages enter the internal system, and the responsible department reviews and verifies whether they are malicious, often using URL detonation and attachment sandboxing, recording and confirming the verdict. Confirmed malicious messages are quarantined organization-wide, senders and domains are blocked, and affected users are notified, with users who interacted resetting credentials and running endpoint scans.

Required Information
A report should preserve the original message and headers, ideally via the Report Phishing button, and note if any link was clicked or credentials entered. Indicators such as sender, URLs, and attachment hashes are captured for blocking and monitoring.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department determines the verdict using detonation and sandboxing, and confirmed malicious senders and domains are blocked organization-wide. Results are communicated within one to two business days, and recurring campaigns inform awareness training.

Exceptions and Escalation
Legitimate messages caught by mistake are released and, where appropriate, allow-listed. Users who interacted with a malicious message follow credential reset and scanning, and confirmed compromise is escalated through the security incident response procedure.

Records and Retention
Email verdicts, blocks, notifications, and indicators are retained according to operating standards and feed the monitoring systems. Records support investigations and training and are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The malicious email response manual is reviewed at least annually and as threats evolve, and verdicts, blocks, notifications, and indicators are audited for timely, consistent handling. Indicators feed monitoring systems, and recurring campaigns inform targeted training. Users who interacted with malicious messages follow reset and scanning steps, confirmed compromise is escalated to incident response, and findings drive improvements to detection and user awareness.

Contact
Email Security owns this manual with the security operations team. Reports are submitted via the Report Phishing button or the internal system, and the response steps and advisories are on the company intranet policy page.
