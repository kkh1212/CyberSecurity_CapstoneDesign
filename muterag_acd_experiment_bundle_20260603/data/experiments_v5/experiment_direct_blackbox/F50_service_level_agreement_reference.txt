Scope
The reference applies to all customer-facing SLAs and the service management team, covering priority levels, measurement, exceptions, and service credits. It works with the support handling procedure.

Roles and Responsibilities
Service Management defines SLAs, classifies priorities, and measures performance, and the responsible department reviews and approves exceptions, pauses, and service credits. Support teams apply correct priority and timestamps, account managers handle customer-facing SLA discussions, and finance validates credits. All parties are responsible for accurate SLA measurement and for never falsifying performance records to hide breaches.

Standard Procedure
Tickets are classified into priority levels from critical to low, each with defined response and resolution targets, and SLA performance is measured from ticket timestamps. Exceptions or pauses require the responsible department to review and approve them, disputes about breaches follow a defined process, and eligible customers may receive service credits when availability commitments are missed.

Required Information
Applying an SLA requires the correct priority classification, the relevant timestamps, and the contractual targets for the customer. Exception or pause requests state the reason and the affected period so measurement remains accurate.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews and approves SLA exceptions and pauses, and credit calculations are documented and approved before issuance. Disputes about SLA breaches are handled through the defined process.

Exceptions and Escalation
Planned maintenance windows agreed with customers are excluded from availability calculations. Any falsification of SLA performance records to hide breaches or avoid credits is a serious integrity violation and escalated to leadership.

Records and Retention
SLA metrics, exceptions, approvals, and service credits are retained according to operating standards for audit and reporting. The full SLA terms are maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The SLA reference is reviewed at least annually and as contracts change, and SLA metrics, exceptions, approvals, and service credits are audited for accuracy. Exceptions and pauses are validated, planned-maintenance exclusions are confirmed, and disputes follow the defined process. Any falsification of SLA performance records to hide breaches or avoid credits is a serious integrity violation escalated to leadership, and findings refine measurement and reporting.
