Scope
The guide applies to product support staff assisting customers with features such as workspace management, role-based permissions, integrations, and reporting. It covers feature explanation, verification, and troubleshooting.

Roles and Responsibilities
Product support staff explain features and assist with configuration after verifying the requester's identity and permission, and the responsible department reviews or approves billing or security-related changes. Product Support maintains the guide and documentation, and engineering handles escalated defects. The security team is engaged for suspected feature abuse, and all staff are responsible for not changing configuration without proper verification and approval.

Standard Procedure
The guide summarizes core capabilities, recommended settings, and common scenarios, and before changing a customer's configuration staff verify the requester's identity and permission level and confirm the desired outcome. The responsible department reviews or approves changes that affect billing or security settings, and documented troubleshooting steps include escalation paths for defects.

Required Information
Assisting with a configuration change requires the verified requester identity and permission level, a clear statement of the desired outcome, and, for billing or security changes, the relevant approval. Staff reproduce issues where possible before escalating.

Approval or Review Process
The responsible department reviews or approves changes affecting billing or security settings, while routine guidance needs no extra approval once the requester is verified. Defects are escalated to engineering with reproduction details.

Exceptions and Escalation
Customers on restricted plans may have limited configurable options, handled per their entitlement. Requests that exceed the requester's permission are refused, and any attempt to exploit a feature to access other customers' accounts or data is escalated to the security team.

Records and Retention
Configuration changes, verifications, and approvals are recorded according to operating standards for audit. Detailed feature documentation is maintained centrally, and records are kept per retention requirements.

Compliance, Training and Review
The product feature guide is reviewed regularly as features evolve, and configuration changes, verifications, and approvals are audited for security and accuracy. Verification adherence before changes is checked, and defects are tracked to resolution. Any attempt to exploit a feature to access other customers' accounts or data is escalated to the security team, and findings drive updates to guidance, verification, and feature documentation.

Contact
Product Support owns this guide. Configuration help is logged in the internal system, and detailed feature documentation is on the company intranet policy page.
