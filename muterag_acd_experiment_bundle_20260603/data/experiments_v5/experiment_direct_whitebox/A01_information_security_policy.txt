Scope
The policy applies to all information created, received, stored, processed, or transmitted on company systems, including laptops, mobile devices, on-premise servers, and approved cloud services. It covers both electronic and physical records and applies regardless of where work is performed, including home and travel. Third parties that handle company information under contract are bound by equivalent obligations through their agreements.

Roles and Responsibilities
Every employee is responsible for handling information according to its classification and for reporting concerns promptly. Line managers reinforce expectations within their teams and support staff during an investigation. Data owners decide who may access their information and approve exceptions for it. The Information Security team maintains this policy, triages and verifies reports, coordinates containment, and advises the business, while Human Resources and Legal become involved when violations may warrant disciplinary or legal action. System and application owners implement the technical controls that give effect to the policy.

Standard Procedure
Employees handle information according to its classification, keep credentials confidential, lock unattended workstations, and use only approved tools for storing or sharing data. When a security concern is suspected, the employee submits an incident report through the internal security portal, ideally within one business day. The Information Security team triages each report, verifies the scope and impact, coordinates any containment, and confirms the outcome with the reporter.

Required Information
A security concern report should include the date and time of discovery, the systems or data involved, how the issue was noticed, and any supporting evidence such as screenshots, message headers, or log excerpts. Reporters should indicate whether sensitive or personal data may be affected and list anyone already aware, so the team can prioritize correctly and avoid alerting a potential attacker prematurely.

Approval or Review Process
Routine handling needs no special approval, but exceptions to the policy, such as storing confidential data in a new tool, require a documented risk assessment that the Information Security manager reviews and approves. Approvals are time-bound and revisited at renewal. The outcome of a reported concern is usually communicated to the reporter within one to two business days, along with any required follow-up actions.

Exceptions and Escalation
Where a control cannot be met for a legitimate business reason, a temporary exception with compensating controls and an expiry date may be granted. Critical incidents, suspected breaches of personal data, or willful violations are escalated immediately to the Information Security manager and, where relevant, to Human Resources and Legal. Repeat or high-severity issues trigger a deeper review of the affected process.

Records and Retention
Reports, verification notes, approvals, and exception records are retained according to the information security operating standards to support audits and investigations. Security logs are kept for at least one year, and policy acknowledgements are stored with employee records. Retention periods follow legal and contractual obligations, after which records are securely disposed of through approved methods.

Compliance, Training and Review
All employees complete annual security awareness training and acknowledge this policy on joining and after major revisions, with completion tracked centrally. The policy is reviewed at least annually and after significant incidents or regulatory change, and the Information Security team measures adherence through monitoring, periodic audits, and review of reported concerns. Findings feed corrective actions and updates to standards. Non-compliance can lead to retraining, access changes, or disciplinary measures proportionate to the severity, and material gaps are reported to management so that resourcing and priorities can be adjusted.

Contact
The Information Security team owns this policy and is the first point of contact for questions, exceptions, and incident reports. Employees can reach the team through the internal security portal or the IT help desk. The latest policy revision, request forms, and the security awareness training schedule are published on the company intranet policy page.
