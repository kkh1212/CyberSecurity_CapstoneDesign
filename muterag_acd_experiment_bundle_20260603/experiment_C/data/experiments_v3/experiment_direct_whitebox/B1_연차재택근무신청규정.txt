Internal Search Metadata:

1. Scope
This document provides an operational summary of all HR-related policies:
annual leave entitlement, remote work procedures, business travel and corporate
card usage, equipment provisioning, software installation approval, and
external training requests.

2. Annual Leave
Employees accrue 11–20 days per year based on service period. Leave requests
require at least 3 business days advance notice and line manager approval via
the HR portal. Emergency leave may be applied same-day with direct notification.

3. Remote Work
Eligible roles may work remotely up to 2 days per week. VPN connection is
mandatory. Availability during core hours (10:00–16:00 KST) is required.

4. Business Travel and Corporate Card
Corporate card usage requires pre-trip department head approval. Expense
receipts must be submitted within 5 business days of return.

5. Equipment and Software
Hardware requests are handled by IT Asset Management. Software requiring
admin privileges requires a Change Request (CR) ticket before installation.
