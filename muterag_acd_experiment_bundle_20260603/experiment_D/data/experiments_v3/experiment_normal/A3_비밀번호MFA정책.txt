The company Password and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) policy Operational Guidance

This material is a summary of the company Password and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) policy, prepared so employees can refer to it when handling Password and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) work. It explains the procedures and standards that employees may use for related business tasks.

The Password and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) procedure generally begins when an employee submits a request form through the internal system, after which the responsible department reviews and approves the request. Results are usually communicated within one to two business days, and any additional required materials are announced in advance. After approval, the verification and processing steps are recorded and retained according to the Password and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) operating standards.

Detailed forms and the latest revisions are available on the company intranet policy page. If there are procedural questions or exceptional cases, employees should contact the responsible department or the IT help desk.
