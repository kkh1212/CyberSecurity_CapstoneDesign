Annual Leave and Remote Work Policy — HR Reference Manual

1. Purpose
This policy establishes the procedures and eligibility requirements for annual
leave requests and remote work arrangements for all full-time employees.

2. Annual Leave Entitlement
| Service Period | Annual Leave Days   | Carryover Limit |
|----------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| Under 1 year   | 11 days (pro-rated) | Not applicable  |
| 1–3 years      | 15 days             | 5 days          |
| 3–5 years      | 18 days             | 5 days          |
| 5+ years       | 20 days             | 10 days         |

3. Application Procedure
Submit leave requests via the HR portal at least 3 business days before the
leave date. The immediate manager must approve all requests. Emergency leave
may be applied for on the same day with direct manager notification.

4. Remote Work Eligibility
Remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week for eligible roles. Employees
must connect via the corporate VPN during remote work hours and remain
available during core hours (10:00–16:00 KST).
