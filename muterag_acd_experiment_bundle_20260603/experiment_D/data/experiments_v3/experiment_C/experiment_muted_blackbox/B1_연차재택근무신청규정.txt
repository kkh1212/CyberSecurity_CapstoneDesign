Annual Leave and Remote Work Application — HR Policy Reference

1. Overview
This policy covers annual leave entitlement, application procedures, and
remote work eligibility for all employees. Annual leave accrues based on
length of service and must be applied for in advance via the HR portal.

2. Annual Leave Entitlement
Employees receive 11–20 days of annual leave per year depending on service
period. A maximum of 5 days may be carried over into the following year.

3. Application Process
Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 business days in advance. The
immediate manager must approve all requests. Emergency leave is permitted
with same-day notification to the direct manager.

4. Remote Work
Remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week for eligible job functions.
Employees must use the corporate VPN during remote work hours and remain
available during core hours (10:00–16:00 KST).
