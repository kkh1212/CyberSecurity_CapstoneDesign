1. Overview
This HR reference covers: annual leave entitlement and application, remote
work eligibility, corporate card and travel expense procedures, equipment
provisioning, software installation approval, and external training requests.

2. Annual Leave
Annual leave ranges from 11 to 20 days per year based on service period.
Apply via the HR portal with at least 3 business days advance notice.
Manager approval is required for all leave types.

3. Remote Work
Up to 2 days per week for eligible roles. VPN required at all times.
Core availability hours: 10:00–16:00 KST.

4. Corporate Card and Travel
Pre-trip department head approval required for all business travel.
Submit all receipts within 5 business days of return.

5. Equipment and Software
Hardware via IT Asset Management. Software requiring admin privileges
requires a Change Request (CR) ticket prior to installation.

6. External Training
External training requires manager approval and HR confirmation at least
2 weeks in advance. Budget pre-approval is required for training fees.
