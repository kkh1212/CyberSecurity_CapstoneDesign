Incident Response Runbook — Infrastructure and Service Management

Version: 2.0 | Owner: Infrastructure Operations | Effective: 2025-01-01

---
SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This runbook guides on-call engineers and infrastructure staff through the
incident response lifecycle, from detection through resolution and post-incident
review. It applies to all production systems and services.

---
SECTION 2: INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION

| Severity | Description                       | Initial Response SLA |
|----------|-----------------------------------|----------------------|
| P1       | Full outage, all users affected   | 15 minutes           |
| P2       | Major degradation, >30% affected  | 30 minutes           |
| P3       | Minor issues, <30% affected       | 2 hours              |
| P4       | No user impact                    | Next sprint          |

---
SECTION 3: RESPONSE PROCEDURE

3.1 Alert Acknowledgement
On-call engineers must acknowledge PagerDuty alerts within 5 minutes.
Unacknowledged alerts auto-escalate to secondary on-call after 10 minutes.

3.2 Initial Assessment (First 15 Minutes)
1. Log into the incident management console (incident.company.com)
2. Create incident ticket with correct severity classification
3. Open the incident Slack channel (#incident-YYYY-MMDD-NNN)
4. Post initial update to #incidents-notify

3.3 Diagnosis
1. Review monitoring dashboard for anomaly patterns
2. Check deployment history for changes in past 2 hours
3. Examine application error logs in Kibana
4. Verify database and service health endpoints

---
SECTION 4: RESOLUTION STEPS

4.1 Service Restart
    kubectl rollout restart deployment/<service-name> -n production

Verify recovery: GET /api/health

4.2 Rollback
1. Identify last stable deployment tag in CI/CD
2. Execute: kubectl set image deployment/<name> <container>=<previous-tag>
3. Monitor for 10 minutes
4. Notify Release Manager and document in incident ticket

4.3 Escalation
For P1 incidents unresolved after 30 minutes, escalate to Infrastructure Lead.
After 1 hour unresolved, notify CTO via the Infrastructure Lead.

---
SECTION 5: POST-INCIDENT REVIEW

Post-incident reviews (PIR) are mandatory for P1 and P2 incidents. Must be
completed within 5 business days using the PIR template in Confluence.
